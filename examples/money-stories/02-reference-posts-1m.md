# Money Stories About Famous People: эталоны 1M+ (2026-11-18)

Правила: `guidelines/money-playbook.md`. Каждый пост набрал больше 1 млн просмотров (данные автора). Видео расшифрованы целиком.
Факты в постах не образец: перед новым постом проверять заново.

## 1. Phil Ivey: выиграл миллионы, казино не платят
- **Видео:** Репортаж о суде (говорящая голова + эксперт по казино + кадры стола), 8:41, полный сюжет
- **Первая фраза:** «They spend millions and millions of dollars on game protection… I'm viewing the casino as my opponent, it's my job to try to exploit weaknesses»
- **Последняя фраза:** «I did nothing wrong, nothing illegal.»
- **Разбор:** Хук: сумма + «paid him, then sued». Видео-строка пересказывает первую мысль ролика. Механика (карты, дилер) объяснена простыми словами без термина. Документ: суд UK 2017. Поворот: «Ivey knew something about its own cards that it didn't.» Вопрос «X или Y?».

```text
Professional poker player Phil Ivey won $9.6 million at the Borgata. The casino paid him, then sued to get it back. At London’s Crockfords, he won another £7.7 million. They refused to pay.

In the video below, Ivey says his job is to play the game, find a weakness in the casino and use it to win. He calls the casino his opponent and points out that it spends millions of dollars protecting its games.

He wanted a specific brand of cards, a Cantonese-speaking dealer and the same decks reused. The casinos agreed. His playing partner, Cheung Yin Sun, asked the dealer to rotate certain cards, presenting it as a matter of luck.

The cards had tiny differences along their backs. Once high-value cards faced one way and the others faced the opposite way, those differences helped identify what was coming next.

Ivey never marked the cards. The dealer handled them. But the casino didn’t understand what those requests were doing to the odds.

He called it an advantage. The casinos called it cheating. In 2017, the UK Supreme Court ruled against him: persuading the dealer to arrange the cards crossed the line.

The casino had approved his requests while expecting to win. Ivey knew something about its own cards that it didn’t.

Was he cheating, or should a casino know its own game before accepting someone’s money?
```

## 2. J. Paul Getty: отказ платить выкуп
- **Видео:** Архив AP: мать похищенного на пресс-конференции, 0:48
- **Первая фраза:** «…we would like to ask through the press… that the family is ready to negotiate»
- **Последняя фраза:** «No, I didn't say the family is ready to pay. I said that the family is ready to negotiate.»
- **Разбор:** Хук: «richest man… refused to pay $17 million… Getty had the money. He simply refused to pay.» Видео во 2-м абзаце с датой («Eight days after…»). Пост строится вокруг одного слова из видео (negotiate vs pay). Самая страшная деталь (ухо) только в тексте. Поворот зеркалит хук. Вопрос «защищал семью или жадный старик?».

```text
In 1973, the richest man in the world, J. Paul Getty, refused to pay a $17 million ransom for his kidnapped 16-year-old grandson. Getty had the money. He simply refused to pay.

Eight days after John Paul Getty III disappeared in Rome, his mother went in front of the cameras and asked the kidnappers to contact the family again. You can see that conversation in the video below.

She said the family was ready to negotiate. When a reporter interpreted that as ready to pay, she immediately corrected him: “No, I didn't say the family is ready to pay. I said the family is ready to negotiate.”

There was a reason she chose those words so carefully. Getty had already made his position clear. He had 14 other grandchildren, and argued that if he paid for one, he could soon have 14 kidnapped grandchildren.

For months, he refused to give in. Then the kidnappers cut off the boy’s ear and mailed it to an Italian newspaper along with a lock of his hair. The ransom was eventually negotiated down and Getty finally agreed to contribute toward his grandson’s release. Five months after he was kidnapped, John Paul Getty III was freed.

Watching his mother’s interview now makes that one word hit differently. The family was ready to negotiate. The man with the money wasn’t ready to pay.

Was Getty protecting the rest of his family, or was he simply a greedy old man who refused to part with his money?
```

## 3. John Malkovich: всё у Мэдоффа
- **Видео:** 60 Minutes, 1:09
- **Первая фраза:** «I found out… I had directed a play in Mexico City… I saw this photograph of Bernie Madoff in handcuffs»
- **Последняя фраза:** «…most people who were ever born will never have a tenth of that… get on with your life and that's it»
- **Разбор:** Хук: карьера в цифрах + потеря + «It was pretty much everything he'd ever made.» Бытовая деталь из видео: сигареты, хотя бросил. Документ: trustee одобрил $670,000. «Что сейчас»: 72 года, Oscar buzz. Вопрос про страдания звезды vs обычных людей.

```text
John Malkovich made over 100 movies, including Con Air, which grossed more than $200 million. When Bernie Madoff was arrested in 2008, Malkovich lost his entire Madoff account, reportedly worth about $2.3 million. It was pretty much everything he'd ever made.

In the video below, he describes the moment he found out.

He had just come home from directing a play in Mexico City when he saw a photo of Madoff in handcuffs.

He walked in and calmly told his wife he was going around the corner to buy a pack of cigarettes. He had quit smoking.

"We have a little problem," he told her. "Bernie Madoff just got arrested."

The court-appointed trustee later approved a claim for only $670,000, reportedly the amount he had originally put in. Everything his money had supposedly earned was gone.

At first, he says, he wanted someone to blame. Then he told himself to shut up. Most people who were ever born will never have a tenth of what he lost.

At 72, he's still working, and his new film is already getting Oscar buzz.

Do you believe a Hollywood star really suffers after losing millions, or is it nothing compared to what regular people go through?
```

## 4. Demi Moore: мать и последние месяцы
- **Видео:** The Drew Barrymore Show, 1:27
- **Первая фраза:** «…I heard that my mother had cancer and was dying»
- **Последняя фраза:** «I feel like I walked away with more of her than I had when I went in.»
- **Разбор:** Хук начинается с самой тёмной детали прошлого, сумма внутри цитаты («for $500»). Видео в 3-м абзаце («she explains why»). Детство и $12.5 млн только в тексте, в ролике момент прощения. Цитата «She never really apologized». Вопрос «а ты бы простил?».

```text
Demi Moore was 15 when a grown man raped her, then asked: "How does it feel to be whored by your mother for $500?"

Decades later, she spent the last 3 and a half months of her mother's life taking care of her.

In the video below, she explains why.

Her mother battled alcoholism and attempted suicide many times. At 12, Demi was digging pills out of her mother's mouth with her fingers.

As a teen, her mother took her to bars so men would notice them. Then one night, an older man they knew was waiting in the apartment with a key.

Demi says she doesn't believe it was a straightforward deal, but her mother gave him access and put her in harm's way.

She left home, dropped out of school and went on to become the highest-paid actress in Hollywood, earning $12.5 million for a single film.

Then she got the call that her mother was dying of cancer. At first, she thought it might be a scam. She went anyway.

"She never really apologized," Demi says.

If your mother had done this to you as a child, would you be able to forgive her?
```

## 5. Christopher Reeve: гонорар против цены беды
- **Видео:** ABC, Barbara Walters, 2:34 (говорят Reeve и Dana)
- **Первая фраза:** «…Dana, my wonderful, wonderful wife… I said, do you still want me this way?»
- **Последняя фраза:** «That thought vanished and has never come back again.»
- **Разбор:** Хук: $250,000 за Супермена против $400,000 в год ухода + «стоит ли жить». Деталь: Robin Williams в костюме русского доктора. Финал: смерть в 52, Dana через 2 года в 44. Вопрос про любовь сегодня.

```text
Christopher Reeve was paid $250,000 to play Superman. After a horse threw him in 1995, his care was expected to cost about $400,000 a year, and he asked his wife if it would be better for him to stop living.

In the video below, months after the accident, he tells Barbara Walters what she said next.

Superman made Reeve a household name. He went on to earn $1 million each for Superman III and IV.

In May 1995, his horse stopped at a jump during a competition in Virginia. Reeve flew over its head, landed on his head and broke his neck. He was paralyzed from the neck down and couldn't breathe without a machine.

Lying in the hospital, he told his wife Dana that maybe it wasn't worth everyone's trouble, and that he should just be allowed to die. She told him: "You're still you, and I love you."

He says the thought disappeared when his kids walked into the room, and it never came back.

One of the first times he laughed again was when his old Juilliard classmate Robin Williams burst into his room dressed as a crazy Russian doctor.

Reeve spent the next nine years fighting for spinal cord research. He died in 2004 at 52. Dana died of lung cancer less than two years later. She was 44.

Is this what real love looks like, or is that kind of love just gone today?
```

## 6. Johnny Depp: $300 млн и альпаки
- **Видео:** Судебная трансляция, показания под присягой, 1:59
- **Первая фраза:** «Mr. Depp, what was your intention with respect to future Pirates movies…»
- **Последняя фраза:** «…I stuck to my guns with a character and it seemed to work.»
- **Разбор:** Хук: старая цитата из суда + свежая новость «This week». Сделка $22.5 млн, решение присяжных 2022. «Что сейчас» с источником (Entertainment Tonight). Вопрос «а ты бы купил билет?».

```text
Johnny Depp once testified he wouldn't play Jack Sparrow again for $300 million and a million alpacas. This week, he said he's open to coming back.

In the video below, he explains under oath why losing that role felt like betrayal.

Depp built Captain Jack Sparrow from scratch. He says Disney executives first hated the character. The five Pirates movies went on to make billions.

In 2018, after Amber Heard wrote a Washington Post column about domestic abuse, Disney dropped him from the franchise. His agent testified he'd had a deal worth a reported $22.5 million for the sixth film.

In court, Depp said he had planned to keep playing Jack until the character got a proper goodbye.

In 2022, a jury sided with Depp in his defamation case against Heard.

This week, he told Entertainment Tonight he's spoken with producer Jerry Bruckheimer, and that the only involvement he wants is "to send them off on a better note."

After everything Disney put him through, would you still buy a ticket to see Depp's last Pirates movie?
```

## 7. Kenneth Copeland: джеты и «демоны»
- **Видео:** Inside Edition, засада у машины, 2:24
- **Первая фраза:** «How are you, sir? We'd just like to ask you about why you don't want to fly commercial»
- **Последняя фраза:** «…He made that airplane so cheap for me. I couldn't help but buy it.»
- **Разбор:** Хук: $760 млн + слова против простых людей. Видео: репортёр ловит его у машины (сюжетная картинка). Детали: 1500 акров, свой аэродром, джет от Tyler Perry. Отрицание «никогда не называл демонами». Поворот: «Most of them have never been on a private jet.» Вопрос «а ты бы дал денег?».

```text
Kenneth Copeland is a preacher reportedly worth up to $760 million. He owns three private jets, and he says regular passengers are the reason he can’t fly commercial.

In the video below, a reporter catches him in his car and asks him about it.

In an old video with another preacher, Copeland explained why he won’t fly with ordinary people. He said he didn’t want to get into “a long tube with a bunch of demons.”

His ministry’s 1,500-acre campus near Dallas–Fort Worth has its own airstrip and hangar. One of his jets is a Gulfstream he bought from Tyler Perry.

When Inside Edition asked how much he paid for it, he said that was none of her business. Then he denied ever calling people demons.

His followers are mostly ordinary people who send donations to his ministry. Most of them have never been on a private jet.

Would you still give money to a pastor who calls people like you demons, or would that be the moment you walk out?
```

## 8. Jamie Oliver: £25 млн и пустой ресторан
- **Видео:** Документальный фильм, 1:55 (ходит по закрытому ресторану)
- **Первая фраза:** «I've been so stressed… you'll see it over on the pillar…» (табличка погибшим ученикам)
- **Последняя фраза:** «…these big cathedrals that you can't fill»
- **Разбор:** Хук: «lost about £25 million of his own money, roughly $30 million. It wasn't enough.» Масштаб: 42 ресторана, £108 млн в год, 1000 уволенных, £83 млн долгов. Другая сторона: £5.2 млн дивидендов. «Что сейчас»: возвращает бренд. Вопрос «а ты бы начал заново?».

```text
Jamie Oliver says he lost about £25 million of his own money, roughly $30 million, trying to save his restaurants. It wasn't enough.

In the video below, he walks through his first restaurant after it closed, and breaks down.

At its peak, his chain had 42 Jamie's Italian restaurants in Britain and turned over £108 million a year.

In 2019, it collapsed. 22 restaurants closed in one day, around 1,000 people lost their jobs, and creditors were left with about £83 million in losses.

Walking through the empty building, he says it feels like a bomb went off and everyone left. He stops at a plaque for two of his former apprentices who died, and can't hold back the tears.

He admits he was good at running one restaurant, but was never really a businessman.

Not everyone felt sorry for him. British papers reported that months before the collapse, he and his wife took £5.2 million in dividends from his media company.

Now he's bringing Jamie's Italian back.

Would you have the courage to start over after losing $30 million, or would you walk away for good?
```
