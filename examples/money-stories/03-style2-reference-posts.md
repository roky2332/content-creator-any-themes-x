He had to sue to get his own house back. Then he sold it for a reported $39 million.

In the video below, Phil tells Piers Morgan what he regrets most: "One time I jumped, two times I was pushed."

1999. He marries Orianne Cevey. They have 2 sons.

2006. They split. 2008, the divorce: a reported £25 million, one of the biggest in British history at the time.

2015. He buys a waterfront mansion in Miami Beach for $33 million.

2016. Phil and Orianne get back together. She moves in.

August 2020. She ends it by text. Weeks later she marries a musician in Las Vegas, then moves him into Phil's house.

She says she won't leave until he renegotiates their divorce.

January 2021. A judge sets a final date. They leave. She puts his gold records and awards up for auction.

Weeks later the house goes under contract.

$47 million to divorce her. $33 million for the house. $100 for one of his gold records.

Was Orianne owed more, or did Phil Collins pay for the same divorce twice?
