# Эталон AI-формата: «удалённые фото» (одобрен автором, 2026-11-07)

Образец структуры для ниши AI / Tech: пост + первый комментарий с промптом.
Анимация к посту: `animations/videos/final/01-photos.mp4` (показывается на экране телефона).

## Пост
```text
My girlfriend got a photo from me last night. I never sent it… Claude Code just found 2,847 photos I deleted from my phone 6 years ago, and they're being sent from my own accounts.

She saw it at 2 am. I was asleep next to her.

I only asked Claude Code to check why my iPhone storage was full again. It found an old cloud app I installed in 2019 and completely forgot about. I was still logged in.

Every photo I ever deleted from my phone was still in there. Deleting them from Photos never deleted them from that cloud.

Including the one I deleted the morning after a trip I never told her about.

Then it checked what had been opening that cloud. A free "AI photo enhancer" I installed in March to fix one blurry picture. It asked me to "connect your cloud for better results". I tapped Connect without reading. That was my mistake.

It wasn't just copying the photos. It was sending them as me:

→ 23 people → through my WhatsApp, from my number
→ my mom, my sister, my girlfriend → between 1 and 3 am
→ 3 image sites → with my first name in the file names
→ last one sent → 40 minutes before I found it

To everyone who got them, I sent those photos myself.

Claude Code removed the app and logged my WhatsApp out of every device. That took 2 minutes. Explaining it is taking much longer.

There's one more thing. Claude Code showed me a photo in that cloud I don't remember taking. It was taken in my apartment. I'm not in it.

She left this morning without saying a word. Her phone is still face down on my nightstand.

How to check what old apps still keep your deleted photos is in the first comment. Save this and check your phone tonight.
```

## Первый комментарий
```text
The prompt I used. Paste it into the Claude app:

"Walk me step by step through checking which apps on my iPhone can see or upload my photos. I'll send screenshots of my settings. Tell me what's risky and what to change today."
```

## Разбор структуры
1. Хук с последствия: «My girlfriend got a photo from me… I never sent it…» + «Claude Code just found» + точное число + многоточие
2. Вторая строка — удар по отношениям: «She saw it at 2 am. I was asleep next to her.»
3. Бытовой вход на телефоне: «storage full again» → забытое облачное приложение
4. Механика одной фразой: удаление из Photos не удаляет из облака
5. Деталь вместо откровенности: «the one I deleted the morning after a trip I never told her about»
6. Ошибка героя: бесплатное AI-приложение, «Connect» без чтения, «That was my mistake.»
7. Поворот: «It was sending them as me» + улики стрелками + время
8. Репутационный удар: «To everyone who got them, I sent those photos myself.»
9. Действия коротко: «That took 2 minutes. Explaining it is taking much longer.»
10. Открытая загадка: фото, которое он не снимал, в его квартире
11. Тревожная финальная сцена: «Her phone is still face down on my nightstand.»
12. CTA → первый комментарий с промптом для приложения Claude
