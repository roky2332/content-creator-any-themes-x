So was Tarantino dodging a fair question, or did the reporter deserve to get shut down?

Five years later she was a Bond villain opposite Roger Moore, and she became one of the most influential icons in music and fashion. Harty never escaped the clip, and it followed him for the rest of his career. He died in 1988 at just 53.
