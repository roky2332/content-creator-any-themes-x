## 2. Taylor: Figueroa Street

**Стиль B «портрет с улицы».** **Видео:** SWU, 18:25, целиком. 0:00–0:09 заставка, 0:09: «All right, Taylor… where'd you grow up?» — первые секунды спокойные, цепляет пост


**Разбор:**
- Хук: плотный абзац-досье в настоящем времени: имя, возраст, с какого возраста, что, где, сколько, как она живёт, и финальная фраза о том, КАК она об этом говорит
- Дальше по темам, а не по порядку видео: наркотики → деньги → нож и дверь, которая открывается изнутри → отец («if you listen…») → одиночество → мечта стать актрисой → совет «choose a different career»
- Почти всё из видео, коротко и рублено, без цитат-абзацев
- Предпоследний блок: авторская рефлексия о том, КАК она говорит («She never raises her voice… That flatness is what stays with you»)
- Финал без вопроса: «She's still out there.»
- ОШИБКА в посте: в видео ей 20 (2:17), начала в 15, «five years». В посте 28 и «с 15». Возраст и цифры всегда сверять с видео

## 3. Harmonie / Chyna: Figueroa Street

**Стиль B «портрет с улицы».** **Видео:** SWU «Prostitute Chyna», 17:16, целиком. Начинается с титульной заставки SWU

```text
Her name is Harmonie. She goes by Chyna on the streets. She is 13 years old, pregnant, and standing on Figueroa Street in South Central Los Angeles at night, telling a stranger with a camera that this is just how her life is now. She ran away from home. This is not a third-world country. This is Los Angeles, California. One of the wealthiest cities on earth. And this 13-year-old girl is working the same stretch of street where, according to LAPD vice investigators, the youngest girl ever rescued was 9 years old.

Figueroa Street - called "the Blade" by everyone who works it - is one of the most notorious sex trafficking corridors in the United States. It operates completely in the open. Traffickers stand on side streets watching. Men idle their cars at the curb. Girls as young as 12 and 13 walk up and down the sidewalk at 2 in the morning. And Harmonie was one of them.

She didn't come from nowhere. Runaways don't just appear on Figueroa Street by accident. The pipeline is almost always the same: a broken home, an absent or abusive parent, a foster system that failed to hold her, and then one person - a pimp, a "boyfriend," a friend who seemed cool - who showed her that the street was an option. That this was a way to survive. By the time a 13-year-old is working Figueroa, there have already been a hundred moments where an adult could have intervened and didn't.

What makes this video so hard to watch is not what she says. It's how she says it.

She's not hysterical. She's not begging for help. She talks like a person who has already processed this, already accepted it, already stopped expecting anything different. The flatness in a 13-year-old's voice when she describes her life on the street - that is what breaks you. Because that flatness is what surviving looks like when you're 13 and you've run out of adults to trust.

In the follow-up video, she is 14. Still pregnant. Legal problems keeping her stuck in Los Angeles, away from whatever family she has left. Still no stable place to live.

The original interview has over 6 million views.

Six million people watched a 13-year-old pregnant girl on Figueroa Street.

And she is still out there.
```

**Разбор:**
- Каркас B в чистом виде: «Her name is…» → «This is not a third-world country. This is Los Angeles» + внешний факт (LAPD, самой младшей спасённой было 9) → как устроена улица («the Blade») → «She didn't come from nowhere» (путь, где взрослые не вмешались) → «What makes this video so hard to watch is not what she says. It's how she says it» → follow-up → число просмотров → «Six million people watched…» → «And she is still out there.»
- Пост почти НЕ пересказывает видео: держится на контексте и рефлексии
- ОШИБКА: приложенное видео не совпадает с постом. В видео Chyna 16 лет, у неё сын, мать убил отец, начала в 13, работает на сутенёра, младшая сестра 14 лет тоже на улице, хочет открыть салон ногтей. В посте 13 лет и беременна. Для новых постов: детали героя только из её видео
- Несовершеннолетние: в новых постах не писать «selling herself» о ребёнке. Ребёнок — жертва эксплуатации, без сексуальных подробностей

## 5. Jay Katz: убийство отца

**Стиль A «досье журналиста» (тёмная история).** **Видео:** Нарезка 7:03 с вшитыми субтитрами. 0:00 сразу внутри истории («So I drove back to his house…»). Обрывается на клиффхэнгере: он стоит в кухне с пистолетом, родители ещё не знают

```text

In the video below, he walks through that afternoon minute by minute, from the bed to the kitchen doorway.

Both of them were doctors. According to Jay, his mother started bringing him into her sex life with her boyfriends when he was about 7 and told him it wasn't abuse because she was there to supervise. He says his stepmother abused him too, and that his dad let it happen.

None of that made the news in 1991. One headline called him manic-depressive, and police said they knew of no motive. It's his word against two people who can't answer. He pleaded guilty, and says he got 100 years but walked out after 10.

Then the brother of the man he killed invited him to a family wedding, paid his way from Oman and slept on the floor so Jay could have the bed. In India, his grandmother just loved him. "I broke my own soul and it shattered."

Was Jay a victim who finally broke, or a killer with a story nobody can check?
```

**Разбор:**
- Хук: сцена в прошедшем времени, год и место первыми, кинематографично по секундам: кровать, .38 и 200 Percocet → отец зовёт смотреть кино → кухня → пуля через руку мачехи → «Jay watched his eyes glaze over». Пост показывает ТО, ЧЕГО НЕТ в видео (видео обрывается раньше)
- Короткий абзац о том, как он рассказывает («He tells it calmly, then stops to cry»)
- Отсылка к видео с точным обещанием: «walks through that afternoon minute by minute, from the bed to the kitchen doorway» — совпадает с границами нарезки
- Его версия (насилие с 7 лет) против записи: «None of that made the news in 1991… police said they knew of no motive. It's his word against two people who can't answer»
- Поворот: брат убитого позвал его на свадьбу и спал на полу. Цитата-финал «I broke my own soul and it shattered»
- Вопрос о доверии: «victim who finally broke, or a killer with a story nobody can check?»
